## 4. Open items for the operator

1. External shop vs /collection: /shop, /online-store, /items, /jewelry currently sell
   products; new site delegates sales to Lightspeed (SHOP_URL). Choose internal vs external.
2. Blog disposition: 55 /post/* URLs — blanket redirect or curate.
3. Policy pages: privacy/return/shipping have no new home.
4. /bio double-hop: Wix already 301s /bio -> /about-rachel-pierce; recommend redirecting
   /bio directly to /story to avoid a chained hop.
5. Apex->www: live site canonicalizes to www; confirm new site canonical host.
